Connected apps

Let Penguin use your apps and accounts.
Google CalendarNot connected

Ask what's on your calendar, add events, move them or delete them. Deleting always asks you to confirm first.

  • Access is limited to events on your primary calendar
  • The sign-in token stays on this laptop
YouTubeNot connected

Say "play a video about GST filing on YouTube", "search YouTube for …", "which channels am I subscribed to". Videos open in the Chrome or Edge profile signed in with your account. This is the same Google account as YouTube Music: connecting or disconnecting one does the same for the other.

  • Read-only access to your YouTube library (playlists, subscriptions)
  • The sign-in token stays on this laptop
Google TasksNot connected

Say "add a task to call the bank tomorrow", "what are my tasks", "mark the bank task done". Uses its own Google account, separate from Calendar and YouTube Music.

  • Access is limited to your Google Tasks
  • The sign-in token stays on this laptop
GmailNot connected

Say "any unread emails", "read the one from the bank", "email Ravi that I'll be late". Penguin only saves a draft unless you clearly ask it to send. Uses its own Google account.

  • Can read, draft, send and mark mail as read; it cannot permanently delete
  • The sign-in token stays on this laptop
Personal FTNot connected

Say "Spent 1000 rupees on pani puri today from SBI" and Penguin adds it to Personal FT the same way Quick Add does: it picks the category and puts it under the right bank. Connect signs you in with Google on the PFT site.

  • Penguin gets its own session, separate from your browser login
  • It can add expenses as you; disconnect any time
YouTube MusicNot connected

Play any song, artist or album by voice, and your own playlists and liked songs. Signing in with Google picks the Chrome or Edge profile for that account, so your Premium plan (no ads) applies.

  • Its own Google account, separate from Calendar; read-only access to your playlists
  • The sign-in token stays on this laptop
